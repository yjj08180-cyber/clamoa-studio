# 클라모아 피드 스튜디오

Use ChatGPT Image as the default generator. Compose full-bleed portrait 4:5 individual stills. Deliver the quantity requested. A multi-panel board is a separate review aid.

## 시즌과 주제
Verify the requested season with first-party runway notes, colour forecasts and dated fashion coverage. Distinguish forecast colours, observed runway trends, measured demand and the studio’s own invented scene. Record source, date/season, and the styling choice each signal drives. Change sources and palettes when the requested season changes.

## 한 컷 설계
For each image specify the runway cue, dominant colour, subject, surprising physical relationship, lighting, camera distance and practical logo treatment. Use reference photographs to identify abstract qualities such as density, texture or contrast. Invent a fresh setting, gesture, framing and object relationship. Work across fashion portraiture, beauty, constructed still life and visual jokes. Use the occasional bee only when it adds meaning. Alternate sparse and dense frames, wide and macro crops. Use Korean to present results.

## 브랜드 적용
Prefer a user-supplied original wordmark. Use assets/wordmark-guide.png as a visual reconstruction for exploratory work when the original is unavailable. The distinctive mark is six lowercase letters with heavy geometric strokes, a tall l and single-storey a. Show it to the generator as an image reference. Specify one logo on a visible material face. Describe its physical process: tiny white screen print on patent shoes, shallow tone-on-tone blind deboss on pebbled leather, tonal weaving on a garment label, frosted etching on glass, fine engraving on metal. Keep the mark integrated with perspective and lighting and leave room around it. For master-art accuracy, use the supplied master in a deterministic placement step before final production.

## 생성과 검수
If the user requests an interview, first collect preferred and rejected references and one concrete reason. For a production request with an established direction, proceed. Give every image a unique scene brief, create one image per call and track output order. Preserve the approved source logo during parallel generation; avoid relying on recent-image ordering across jobs. Return only verified outputs. Inspect a combined contact sheet for near duplicates, framing, unexpected type, spelling and visible placement. Inspect suspect logos at full scale. Regenerate failed frames and distinguish mockup typography from verified master artwork. Deliver individual images and, for a large series, provide an indexed overview to aid selection.
